About this prototype

How it works

Your recording is sent to our server and passed to ElevenLabs Speech-to-Speech. Words, timing, pauses and emotion stay the same — only the voice changes. Nothing is transcribed or rewritten.

Privacy

We don't save recordings in this application — audio is processed in memory and never written to disk. Audio is sent to ElevenLabs for voice processing and is subject to their processing policies.

Best results

Speak clearly in a quiet room, 5–15 seconds at a time (up to 30). English, Hindi and Hinglish work well with the multilingual model. Free-tier processing capacity is limited.
